# Adversarial FIT Review — Verdict Report

**Scope note:** All evidence verified against the repo at `/mnt/agent-workspace/newt-agent` (HEAD `6b7d780`). Line references are to `newt-tui/src/lib.rs` (8,822 lines) unless prefixed; the plan's bare "lib.rs" citations resolve there, not `newt-core/src/lib.rs` (49 lines) — the plan should disambiguate.

1. **REFUTED — "Phase 9.7 is in flight."** Steps 9.6–9.9 exist only as roadmap text (commit `743ce2a`, "docs(roadmap): Steps 9.6–9.9 — agentic loop ACP exposure (#213)"). No implementation: `chat_complete` and `openai_chat_complete` still live in newt-tui/src/lib.rs:3571 and :4158, `newt-core/src/` has no `agentic/` module, `newt-eval/src/` has no `agentic_mode`, and there are no step-9.x branches or open PRs. **Correction:** the gating direction (loop-touching work after 9.7) is right and matches ROADMAP:552-581, but the plan must state that ~9 of its PRs (A5-A7, B1-B5 except B2, C4-C5) are blocked behind *unstarted* work — and should either make landing 9.7 the explicit first deliverable of this effort or re-order to maximize the pre-9.7 runway (A1-A4, B2, C1-C3, D-prep).

2. **CONFIRMED — Phase-numbering collision, but the proposed fix breaks roadmap ordering.** The decision doc does propose steps 15.1-15.6 (docs/decisions/conversation_context_architecture.md:363-374) colliding with Phase 15 Role Profiles (docs/ROADMAP.md:896); Phases 17+ are free (roadmap ends at Phase 16, ROADMAP:958). **FIT-PROBLEM:** assigning WS-A=17, WS-B=18, WS-C/WS-D=19 while declaring the dependency spine "D1 → B1 → B2.." makes Phase 19 steps prerequisites of Phase 18 — defeating the roadmap's stated branch-sort ordering ("the leading number lets `git branch -l 'step-*' | sort` produce the roadmap order", ROADMAP:28-30), and stuffing two workstreams into one phase number is incoherent. **Fix:** fold D1/D2 in as the first two steps of the WS-B phase (they exist only to enable WS-B), giving Phase 17=WS-A, 18=WS-D+WS-B, 19=WS-C.

3. **FIT-PROBLEM — the rusqlite/FTS5 dependency argument is only partially honest.** Three gaps: (a) the "relational queries we also need — lineage, MRU-by-last-active, tip-projection" justification leans on hermes's architecture, not newt's needs: newt's compression mutates provider-internal history (newt-core/src/memory.rs:787-860) and never forks conversation records, so nothing would ever populate `parent_id`; (b) the argument omits that newt **prunes to 100 conversations per workspace by default** (newt-core/src/config.rs:158-163; conversation.rs:217-231) — inside the plan's own "brute-force acceptable below ~200" threshold, hollowing out the scale rationale at default settings; (c) MRU-by-last-active already exists — see finding 7. **Fix:** rebase the justification on the honest wins: per-turn append currently rewrites the entire pretty-JSON record (conversation.rs:119-127 with save_record:200-215), tool-event indexing, and snippet/ranking quality; and explicitly decide the retention policy — durable cross-session recall is incompatible with silently pruning to 100, and changing that cap is itself a behavior change that needs arguing in A1.

4. **FIT-PROBLEM — `parent_id` lineage + tip-projection is over-engineering by the repo's own standard.** No newt mechanism creates child conversations (see finding 3a; `/conversation` supports only list/show/restore/rename/delete, lib.rs:2002-2029). The roadmap's own norm is explicit YAGNI ("do **not** introduce an `InferenceBackend` trait at this step (YAGNI: there is currently one implementor)", ROADMAP:558-560). **Fix:** cut `parent_id` and tip-projection from the A1 schema; add them in the future PR that introduces an actual branching/compaction-fork feature. This also simplifies the A/B "seam" item.

5. **FIT-PROBLEM — A1's ~500-line estimate is implausible under the coverage gate.** A1 replaces conversation.rs (325 lines) **plus** its 289-line, 14-test integration suite (newt-core/tests/conversation_store.rs) **plus** adds schema, schema-diff migration, WAL fallback, one-time JSON import, and rewiring — all while holding the 80% floor (justfile:131 `floor=80`; ROADMAP:16-17). Realistic size: 900-1,300 lines. Compare: the roadmap budgets ~450 lines for 9.7, a pure code *move*. The summary claim "all within the 100-500 line discipline" is also not a written rule anywhere in ROADMAP.md — it's an inferred norm from the per-step "Estimated diff" fields (~100-450). **Fix:** split A1 into A1a (SQLite store + schema + WAL fallback + tests) and A1b (JSON import + old-path deletion); B3 (~450 for rewiring trim sites plus wiremock loop tests) deserves the same skepticism — budget ~700 or split assembly from boundary logic.

6. **REFUTED — arithmetic: "Total: ~16 PRs."** A1-A7 + B1-B5 + C1-C5 + D1-D2 = **19**. With finding 5's splits it's 20+. Correct the count or cut scope (findings 4 and 12 each remove/shrink a PR).

7. **REFUTED — gap-matrix row "MRU by last_active … newt prunes/lists by record timestamps only."** `updated_at_unix_nanos` is bumped on every `append_turn` (conversation.rs:122) and drives ordering (`oldest_records_first`, conversation.rs:278-283), so record timestamp ≈ last-active already. The hermes `MAX(message ts)` derivation solves a problem newt doesn't have. **Correction:** reclassify HAVE; the only real defect is that `rename` also bumps `updated_at` (conversation.rs:154-159), making a renamed conversation look active — a 3-line fix, not a schema feature.

8. **FIT-PROBLEM — B1 has a hidden crate-boundary prerequisite.** Budget unification wants newt-core thresholds to read `safe_context`/`max_ok_input`, but `CapabilityEntry` and the capability cache live in **newt-tui** (newt-tui/src/probe.rs:73-108; zero references in newt-core), newt-core cannot depend on newt-tui, and Step 9.7 moves only `ChatCtx`/`chat_complete`/`execute_tool` — not probe.rs (ROADMAP:555-562). **Fix:** either (lean, recommended) inject resolved budget values at provider construction from the TUI, mirroring the existing `with_summarizer` injection pattern (lib.rs:1263-1314), or move the capability-cache types into newt-core and bump B1's estimate accordingly.

9. **MISSING — auto-resume vs. eval/ACP ephemerality.** The decision doc the plan builds on *requires* ephemeral runs for graded evals: "`--ephemeral` / `--no-resume` flag and the `NEWT_CONVERSATION_ID` env var override it (eval cases in `newt-eval/` **must** run ephemeral so ambient journals never leak into a graded run)" (conversation_context_architecture.md:~98-103). A7 lists only `/new` and `[context] resume = false`. This matters doubly because CLAUDE.md declares the eval/CI gates "must be honest. Do not game them." **Fix:** A7 must include the `--ephemeral` flag + env override and assert the eval runner defaults to ephemeral.

10. **MISSING — the "limitations 1-13" numbering resolves to nothing in-repo.** The plan cites "fixes limitations 2, 3, 6, 9", "limitation 10", "limitation 13", but no enumerated limitations list exists anywhere in docs/ (grep finds "limitation" only in docs/decisions/mesh_integration.md). It presumably lives in the out-of-repo hermes analysis. For a plan that becomes roadmap phases, that's an unresolvable cross-reference. **Fix:** inline the limitation list into the first PR's doc, or replace each with the concrete file:line it stands for.

11. **FIT-PROBLEM — WS-A silently reverses two accepted decisions but only commits to fixing the doc's *numbering*.** The decision doc chose `FileStore` `turns.jsonl` as "Zero deps, offline. **The bootstrap floor.**" and `ConversationStore` as a *trait* with a `JournalProvider` seam (conversation_context_architecture.md:318-344). The plan's choices (SQLite; concrete struct) are arguably *more* charter-aligned (README:17 "opinionated, not extensible"; ROADMAP:558 YAGNI), but they amend a checked-in decision record. **Fix:** the first WS-A PR must amend/supersede the decision doc for storage and the deferred trait — and state what the Phase-16 `MeshStore`/`LayeredStore` design (doc:330-340; open issues #228-#233) does instead, since it was specified against the trait.

12. **REFUTED — C3 mischaracterizes the code and is oversized.** "Replace `add_note`'s string-name routing hack with a proper trait method": the trait method **already exists** (`MemoryProvider::add_note` default-bails, newt-core/src/memory.rs:177-179); the hack is only the `name() == "note_store"` lookup in `MemoryManager::add_note` (memory.rs:307-316). **Correction:** C3 is a ~20-line cleanup (try each provider, return first `Ok`), not a 150-line PR — fold it into C1.

13. **FIT-PROBLEM (minor) — internal inconsistency on concurrency.** WS-A skips write-retry machinery because of a "single-writer TUI," while WS-C justifies file locking because "newt allows concurrent sessions per workspace" — and the code agrees with WS-C ("Two concurrent newt processes mint distinct ids", conversation.rs:296-299), which means two writers on one shared `~/.newt/conversations.db`. **Fix:** one `busy_timeout` pragma in A1. Lean, honest, no convoy machinery.

14. **CONFIRMED (with an honesty nit) — the "lean ~6-section template" already mostly exists in newt.** The `Summarizing` provider ships a 5-section template (Active Task / Completed Actions / Key Decisions / Relevant Files / Critical Context, memory.rs:816-821). Presenting WS-B's template as an adaptation *from hermes's 14 sections* miscredits it. **Fix:** state that WS-B keeps newt's existing template, adding only the verbatim-Active-Task rule, the `[REFERENCE ONLY]`/end-marker framing, and the In-Progress slot.

15. **MISSING — D2's dependency on WS-A schema is unsequenced.** "Consume real usage on restore" (fixing memory.rs:543 and :911-916 chars/4 re-estimation) requires turns to *carry* token counts, but `ConversationTurn` is `(user, assistant)` only (conversation.rs:9-12); real usage lands in the store via A1/A6. The sequencing summary ("D1 → B1 → B2..B5; A1-A4 parallel; A5-A7, C4-C5 after 9.7") never places D2. **Fix:** declare D2 dependent on A6 (or put a `tokens` column in the A1 schema from day one and feed it from A6).

16. **CONFIRMED — constraint compliance on local-first / no-hosted-deps / leanness is genuine.** Skipping Honcho and hosted providers, aux-model chains, plugin engines, the background review fork, the dual-write store, the gateway state machines, and the 14-section/`max_iterations=9999` machinery all match README:7-17 ("local-first… no cloud bytes leave your machine… opinionated, not extensible") and the confined-loop philosophy (`max_tool_rounds` cap, lib.rs:3955-3968). The "Do NOT copy" section is the strongest part of the plan. blake3 for A2 is already in-tree (newt-identity/Cargo.toml:16) though it becomes a *new direct dep of newt-core* — per ROADMAP:24 ("No new dependency added without justification in the PR body") A2's PR body should say so explicitly rather than relying on "rusqlite is the one new dep."

17. **CONFIRMED — the factual floor of the gap matrix holds.** Spot-checked ~25 load-bearing citations; all verify: discard-only trim with placeholder (lib.rs:2655-2718), pre-send guard wired to `max_ok_input`/`safe_context` (lib.rs:3624, :3692-3706), chars/4 everywhere (lib.rs:2688-2694; memory.rs:511-514, :543), `repair_orphaned_tool_calls` two-pass (lib.rs:2791+), Summarizing's placeholder/abort-restore/anti-thrash/`prev_summary` wipe-on-restore (memory.rs:835-840, :824-832, :779-784, :916-920), blocking summarizer inside `sync_turn` vs the trait's non-blocking contract (lib.rs:1290-1305 vs memory.rs:142-144), frozen NOTES snapshot explicitly "Modelled on hermes-agent's MemoryStore" (memory.rs:562-571), hard-fail over-budget add (memory.rs:606-617), human-only `/remember` (lib.rs:1407-1414), fresh conversation id every launch (lib.rs:1175), `(user, assistant)`-only persistence (conversation.rs:9-12; lib.rs:2268-2285), UUIDv5 path keying (conversation.rs:70-74), probe ratchet with confidence + persisted parsed-400 limits (newt-tui/src/probe.rs:126-189), dead `prefetch` surface (memory.rs:125-127, no overrides), `on_session_end` no-op everywhere (memory.rs:167, :293), and the 80% floor (justfile:131; CLAUDE.md ratchet history).

**Bottom line:** the plan's constraint instincts (local-first, no hosted deps, refuse hermes's architecture, take its algorithms) are sound and its code-level homework is real. The fixable defects are: a stale "in flight" sequencing premise (1), a phase-numbering scheme that contradicts its own dependency spine (2), a dependency argument propped up by two needs newt doesn't have — lineage and scale — while ignoring the 100-conversation prune cap (3, 4), one badly underestimated PR (5), a miscounted total (6), one wrong gap-matrix row (7), a crate-boundary blocker in B1 (8), and three missing reconciliations: eval ephemerality (9), the phantom limitations index (10), and superseding the decision doc it quietly overrules (11).
