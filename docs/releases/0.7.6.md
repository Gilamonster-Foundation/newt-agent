## Notes

- **Methodology:** First release under the **honesty classifier** (a run counts only if the model made a real tool-call attempt on ≥25/30 tasks) **+ weights-digest pinning**. Three models were quarantined rather than banked with a misleadingly-low number.
- **Framing:** A **re-baseline under stricter measurement**, *not* a raw champion-defeat — the honesty floor and digest pinning are new this release, so 0.7.6 numbers are not directly comparable to earlier unclassified/unpinned ones (e.g. qwen3.6 26.7% vs the prior 20%).
- **Caveats:** tb-30 is a 30-task sample; per-model numbers carry ~±10 pp run-to-run variance. On the DGX Spark the agentic ceiling is ~48B — dense ≥70B and the 120B MoE load but generate ≤5.6 t/s, too slow to drive (see the survey), so they are excluded, not failed.
