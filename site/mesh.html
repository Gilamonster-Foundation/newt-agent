How Newt sessions dock across machines

A hub can list, mirror and steer sessions running on another of the operator's own machines. The session never moves: the host stays the only writer. Step through discovery, pairing, approval, mirroring, injecting and revoking.

Placeholders only. Types come from newt-mesh/src/dock.rs. The transport lives in the sibling agent-mesh repo, and newt-mesh is built outside the default workspace. Keys: left/right arrows step, space plays or pauses.

What this does not show

  • Everything is the same operator with one shared root key. Cross-operator pairing is future work.
  • The six-word key mnemonic is a cross-check of a public key, not a two-party SAS.
  • Nothing migrates between hosts. The hub mirrors and injects.
  • Caveats do not travel with a dock request and are not enforced on the remote side.
  • No TLA+ spec covers the mesh.
