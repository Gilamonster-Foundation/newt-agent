How agent-frame records what happened

agent-frame is the pure kernel under Newt's memory: every record is named by a hash of its content, and every derived record names the records it came from. Step through one tool output being recorded, cut down, verified and projected into a request.

Shapes are illustrative. Types come from agent-frame/src/ and agent-harness/src/. Keys: left/right arrows step, space plays or pauses.

What this does not show

  • This is not "infinite context" or lossless memory. Elision is checked by recomputing from the source bytes.
  • A content id proves these bytes hash here. It does not prove an agent may read them.
  • Rows from before encoding v2 have no source attribution.
  • agent-frame is a library, not a service. It has no storage of its own; agent-harness supplies the store.
